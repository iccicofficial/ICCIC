Privacy & Cookies

Privacy & cookies notice

How this website handles your data and the third-party services it uses.

Last updated: June 2026

Who we are

This is the official website of the 6th International Conference on Cognitive & Intelligent Computing (ICCIC-2026), hosted by Vasavi College of Engineering, Hyderabad, India. For any privacy query, contact convenericcic@gmail.com.

What we collect

This site is a static, informational website. We do not run accounts, logins, or on-site payment. We only receive personal data that you choose to send us — for example, when you email or call the conference secretariat, submit a paper through Microsoft CMT, or register for the conference. That may include your name, affiliation, email, phone number and submission details, used solely to organise the conference, manage the review process, and communicate with you.

Cookies & third-party services

This website sets no first-party tracking cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. It does, however, load a small number of third-party services that may set cookies or receive your IP address:

  • Google Fonts — web fonts are loaded from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google Maps — the venue page embeds an interactive map; Google may set cookies when the map loads.
  • GitHub Pages — this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Microsoft CMT — paper submission and peer review are handled on Microsoft’s separate CMT platform, which has its own privacy terms.

External links (Springer, Microsoft CMT, university and partner pages) are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies.

How we use and share data

We use the data you provide only to run ICCIC-2026 and its review and registration processes. We do not sell your personal data. Submission data may be shared with assigned reviewers (under double-blind review) and, for accepted papers, with the proceedings publisher for publication. Proceedings for ICCIC-2026 will be submitted to Springer for publication, subject to approval, consistent with previous editions.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data you have shared with the conference by emailing convenericcic@gmail.com. We handle personal data in line with applicable data-protection law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the conference is organised. The latest version will always appear on this page.
