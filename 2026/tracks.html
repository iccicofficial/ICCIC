Tracks & Topics

Four tracks at the frontier of intelligence

Original, unpublished research is invited across the following themes and the broad spectrum of cognitive & intelligent computing.

1

Computational Intelligence & Neural Networks

Artificial neural networks, deep & reinforcement learning, evolutionary computation, genetic algorithms, fuzzy logic and swarm intelligence.

2

Cognitive Computing

Systems that mimic human reasoning — knowledge representation, cognitive architectures, perception, attention and decision-making.

3

Machine Learning & Intelligent Robots

Supervised & unsupervised learning, autonomous systems, robotics, perception, planning and human-robot interaction.

4

Natural Language Learning

Natural language processing, large language models, speech, semantics, dialogue systems and multilingual understanding.
