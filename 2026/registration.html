What's included & how to register

  • Registration includes the proceedings, a registration kit, and tea/coffee & lunch during the conference period (physical attendees only).
  • At least one author of each accepted paper must register and present the paper for it to be included in the proceedings.
  • Online (virtual) attendees do not receive registration kits, meals, or any discounts.
  • Accommodation is not included in the registration fee and can be requested separately.
  • Payment links open after the acceptance notification; payment details are shared by email.

*Subject to presentation. For exact fees and payment details, contact convenericcic@gmail.com.
