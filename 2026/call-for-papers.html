Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must report original, unpublished research not under review elsewhere.
  • Follow the Springer manuscript format and length guidelines.
  • Each submission undergoes double-blind peer review via Microsoft CMT.
  • At least one author of an accepted paper must register and present.
  • Plagiarism / similarity must be within accepted limits.
