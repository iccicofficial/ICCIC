About the Conference

Where cognition meets computation

A global forum advancing intelligent computing, cognition and cyber-physical systems for smart, sustainable futures.

The International Conference on Cognitive & Intelligent Computing (ICCIC) is a forum of scientists, researchers and civic bodies to discuss and exchange experimental and theoretical results across smart cities, intelligent computing, cognition and cyber-physical systems.

Now in its sixth edition, ICCIC-2026 focuses on cognitive and intelligent computing technologies that address complex real-world problems — spanning artificial neural networks, deep learning, reinforcement learning, evolutionary computation, genetic algorithms, fuzzy logic and swarm intelligence.

The conference brings together a vibrant international community for keynote plenary talks, peer-reviewed paper presentations and rich networking — all hosted at Vasavi College of Engineering, Hyderabad, and published by Springer in a Scopus-indexed series.

ICCIC has been held every year since 2021 — explore the history & past editions to see how the conference has grown.
